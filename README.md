# Lichling

A tiny Lich King who lives on your desktop: on Windows he walks along the taskbar, on a Mac along the top of your Dock. He sits by a campfire, peeks in now and then and announces WoW: Forever news.

## Install on Windows

1. Download **[Lichling-win-Setup.exe](https://github.com/markus-sanness/lichling-releases/releases/latest/download/Lichling-win-Setup.exe)** and double-click it.
2. Windows will say **"Windows protected your PC"** (the app isn't code-signed). Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You only see this once.

That's it. He appears on your taskbar and starts with Windows from now on.

## Install on Mac

1. Download **[Lichling-mac.dmg](https://github.com/markus-sanness/lichling-releases/releases/download/mac-latest/Lichling-mac.dmg)**, open it and drag **Lichling** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Lichling from Applications. macOS says it can't verify Lichling (it isn't signed by Apple). Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to "Lichling was blocked", click **Open Anyway** and enter your password. You only do this once.

He appears on top of your Dock and starts at login from then on. Needs macOS 13 or newer.

## Using him

- **Click** him to raise Frostmourne; click again to make him leap. **Drag** to pick him up and throw him.
- **His menu:** right-click him, or click the snowflake (next to the clock on Windows, in the menu bar on a Mac).
- **Updates:** when there's a new version he tells you, and his menu shows **Update to x.y.z**. Click it and he restarts on the new version.
- He stays out of the way of games: while a full-screen game is in front he hides and doesn't draw anything.
- **Start with Windows** (on a Mac, **Start at Login**) in his menu turns autostart off. **Quit Lichling** closes him.
- **Uninstall:** on Windows, Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Lichling. On a Mac, pick **Quit Lichling** in his menu, then drag Lichling from Applications to the Trash.
